About CropCare AI

An AI-powered crop disease detection and smart agricultural recommendation system.

Project

AI-Based Crop Disease Detection and Smart Recommendation System using Google Gemini AI — trained knowledge from the PlantVillage dataset covering 38 disease classes across 14 crop species.

Dataset

Built on the PlantVillage Dataset — 54,306 images covering 14 crop species and 38 classes (26 diseases + 12 healthy variants). Reference: Hughes & Salathé, 2015.

Bilingual Support

Full English and Tamil (தமிழ்) language support — including disease names, symptoms, prevention, step-by-step treatment, and management recommendations.

Gemini AI

Uses Google Gemini AI for real-time plant disease detection from leaf images — providing instant, accurate crop and disease identification with confidence scores.

How CropCare AI Works

1

Upload Leaf Image

Take a clear close-up photo of the affected leaf and upload it using the camera or file picker.

2

Gemini AI Analysis

The image is sent to Google Gemini AI which identifies the crop species, disease, and confidence level.

3

Disease Report

Receive a full disease report including symptoms, prevention tips, and a 6-step treatment guide.

4

Take Action

Follow the step-by-step treatment instructions in English or Tamil to treat and save your crop.

⚠️ Disclaimer

CropCare AI is an educational and informational tool. It is designed to assist farmers in identifying potential crop diseases. It does NOT replace professional agricultural advice.

  • Always consult a qualified agricultural extension officer for chemical treatment decisions.
  • Follow locally approved pesticide labels for all dosage and application instructions.
  • AI predictions carry uncertainty — use them as a starting point, not a final diagnosis.
  • Do not apply any pesticide or treatment based solely on this application's output.

Dataset Reference

Hughes, D., Salathé, M. (2015). An open access repository of images on plant health to enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics. arXiv:1511.08060. PlantVillage Dataset — 54,306 plant leaf images across 38 disease classes.
